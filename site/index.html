Project at a glance

Dataset

  • Source: PlantVillage on Kaggle (20,638 images, 15 categories)
  • Five balanced classes, 1,000 images each: Tomato Healthy, Bacterial Spot, Early Blight, Late Blight, and a synthetic Not Leaf class
  • Split 70 / 15 / 15: 3,500 train, 750 validation, 750 test (seed 42)
  • 224 × 224 RGB, ImageNet normalization; flip and rotation augmentation on training data only
